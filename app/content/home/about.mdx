My research, focuses on using interaction provenance to enhance the reproducibility and reusability of interactive visual analysis.
I have a **strong software engineering** background, with experience in developing and maintaining **open-source** systems and libraries.
I am interested in developing better systems and libraries to enable reproducible data analysis.

I am a **PhD candidate** (expecting to graduate in Spring 2024) at the  [Kahlert School of Computing](https://www.cs.utah.edu/) and the [Scientific Computing and Imaging Institue](https://sci.utah.edu/) at the [University of Utah](https://www.utah.edu/). I am advised by [Dr. Alexander Lex](https://vdl.sci.utah.edu/team/lex/) at [Visualization Design Lab](https://vdl.sci.utah.edu/).
